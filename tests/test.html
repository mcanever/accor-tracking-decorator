GA4 _gl regeneration test

The hash inside _gl embeds the current minute and gtag.js only accepts it for about 2 minutes. With debug: true every regeneration is logged in the console with a [GA4] prefix. How to test:

  1. Wait until the "Age" below exceeds ga4LinkerMaxAge (30s in this page).
  2. Click "Decorate a URL now": the console shows [GA4] Regenerated _gl and the hash below changes.
  3. Wait again, then click one of the links above: the console shows [GA4] Re-decorated link on click and the opened tab carries a fresh _gl.
  4. With ga4RefreshInterval set, the hrefs above are also refreshed automatically (hover them to check).
